04 / INTERNATIONAL TRADE UN COMTRADE · ANNUAL MERCHANDISE TRADE

Who buys from Ethiopia?

Explore top reported destinations of Ethiopian merchandise exports using annual trade records. This is separate from exports as a share of GDP, which covers goods and services.

Trade partners are loaded on request to respect public API limits. If the public API cannot provide a complete comparable partner ranking, the website will say so rather than invent a ranking.

UN Comtrade publishes official reported trade observations when available, not live shipping flows. Public preview access is rate-limited and may be incomplete. Explore UN Comtrade ↗

05 / ABOUT THE RESEARCH TRANSPARENT SOURCES · OPEN EXPLORATION

Built to make economic geography accessible.

Ethiopia Economic Atlas is an independent research and visualization initiative. The facility dataset is a frozen November 2026 compilation, while national indicator cards request the latest published data from external sources.

It is an exploratory educational resource—not an official registry, investment recommendation, current operating-status confirmation, or substitute for due diligence.
